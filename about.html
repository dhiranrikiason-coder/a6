Maison Heritage

The Architecture of Metallic Haute Maroquinerie

Founded on Via Monte Napoleone, MetallicTote bridges ancient Tuscan vegetable tanning with avant-garde metallic leafing, creating luxury leather totes of singular sculptural power.

Our Founding Ethos

Reclaiming Slow Artisanal Integrity

In a luxury landscape dominated by mechanized assembly lines and synthetic faux-metallic coatings, MetallicTote was founded as an uncompromising sanctuary for traditional leathercraft.

We source exclusively from heritage family tanneries along the Arno Valley, where full-grain calfskins are slowly drummed with natural chestnut and mimosa tannins for over sixty days to achieve their supple hand-feel and immense tensile strength.

Every piece is individually cut, skived, and hand-stitched by master artisans who view leathercraft not as commercial manufacturing, but as enduring sculptural art.

Hand Saddle Stitching

Our artisans execute traditional double-needle saddle stitching with beeswax-coated French linen thread, ensuring indestructible seam integrity that machine sewing can never match.

Lost-Wax Brass Metallurgy

Custom hardware is cast in solid brass using historic lost-wax investment molds, hand-polished, and electroplated with five microns of heavy palladium and 24K gold.

Limited Edition Curation

To ensure uncompromising quality, our Milanese atelier produces fewer than five hundred pieces per calendar year, each individually numbered and certified in our master ledger.

Milanese Flagship

The Via Monte Napoleone Atelier Salon

We welcome patrons and international collectors to experience our materials firsthand at our Milanese flagship salon. Examine raw leather hides, touch tactile metallic swatches, and witness our artisans at work.

Private salon appointments may be scheduled through our concierge desk, offering dedicated one-on-one time with our design directors to configure bespoke commissions.
