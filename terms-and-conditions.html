2. Bespoke Commissions, Production Timelines, and Deposits

Acquiring a bespoke handcrafted metallic tote bag represents a collaborative artisanal journey requiring intensive manual labor, precision hide selection, and custom metallurgical fabrication. When commissioning a custom tote, clients review and approve detailed digital renderings specifying leather colorway, metallic leafing hue, hardware metallurgy, thread color, and personalized hot-stamped monograms. A non-refundable bespoke deposit of fifty percent (50%) of the total estimated commission price is required prior to hide cutting and hardware casting. Because bespoke leather pieces are tailored to unique personal specifications, production schedules typically span eight to fourteen weeks from initial deposit confirmation, depending upon hide tanning availability and artisanal scheduling. Our atelier concierge provides regular photographic milestone dispatches keeping clients informed as their tote progresses through cutting, skiving, saddle-stitching, and edge-painting.

Clients acknowledge that natural full-grain calfskin possesses inherent organic variations, including subtle grain textural shifts, natural micro-creases, and microscopic pores that reflect authentic animal provenance. Furthermore, vacuum-applied metallic foil finishes and gold leafing exhibit delicate artisanal nuances that distinguish each handcrafted piece from mass-manufactured synthetic foils. These organic variations are celebrated hallmarks of haute maroquinerie and do not constitute manufacturing defects or grounds for commission cancellation. Once custom leather cutting has commenced, client-requested design modifications may incur supplementary artisanal charges and schedule adjustments. Full payment of the remaining fifty percent balance, plus applicable insured shipping and customs fees, must be settled prior to final packaging and dispatch from our Milanese atelier.

Published prices for ready-to-wear collection pieces include Italian Value Added Tax (VAT) where applicable, but exclude international import duties, cross-border customs clearance tariffs, and specialized white-glove transport fees. For international shipments destined outside the European Union, the recipient is the importer of record and assumes full responsibility for satisfying all statutory customs tariffs and import taxes assessed by destination border authorities. We provide comprehensive customs documentation, certified material provenance certificates, and commercial invoices to facilitate expedited international clearance through global customs corridors.

Every tote leaving our Milanese atelier is dispatched via premier insured courier services featuring tamper-evident packaging, secure tracking, and mandatory adult signature verification upon delivery. In the extraordinary event of transit damage or package loss, MetallicTote maintains comprehensive marine and air cargo insurance covering full replacement value. Clients must inspect delivered luxury parcels immediately upon arrival and document any visible exterior transit damage with the courier before signing. Notice of transit-related discrepancies must be communicated to our concierge within forty-eight hours of delivery receipt to initiate immediate insurance claim processing and priority atelier replacement.

5. Lifetime Craftsmanship Warranty, Returns, and Dispute Resolution

Every handcrafted bag bearing the MetallicTote hallmark is accompanied by an unbroken Lifetime Craftsmanship Warranty covering structural seam integrity, saddle-stitching durability, and solid brass hardware function. Should a tote experience seam separation, hardware mechanical failure, or structural lining detachment under normal luxury wear, our Milanese atelier will repair or restore the piece free of charge. This warranty does not cover damage resulting from catastrophic accidents, chemical chemical exposure, abrasive surface scrapes, water submersion, or unauthorized third-party cobbler modifications. Metallic foil finishes naturally evolve a distinguished, softening patina over years of dedicated carry, a cherished characteristic of authentic metallic leathers that is thoroughly supported by our annual atelier re-conditioning and re-glazing services.

Ready-to-wear collection pieces purchased online may be returned for exchange or full refund within fourteen calendar days of receipt, provided the item remains in pristine, unworn condition with all protective films, security tags, dust bags, and serial certificates intact.
